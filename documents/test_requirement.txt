Scraper Tests:
==============
Will test the class primeScraper for the following test cases:
1. Get current prime by calling scrapeCurrentPrimeRate
    a. Successful scraping
    b. Failed scraping - catch the expected exception
2. Get prime changes in date range (using html_prime_full_changes_example.html as an example):
    a. Test for range 1/1/2025 to 1/1/2026 - verify the changes returned are (2025-09-17 & 7.25%, 2025-10-30 & 7.00%, 2025-12-11 & 6.75%), the initial rate should be 7.50% (from 2024-12-20)

    c. Get range of dates starting before the first available prime change (before 1955-08-04) - this should fail on no initial prime value.
